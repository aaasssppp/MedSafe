Welcome to MedSafe

I am your medical report understanding assistant. Here is how to get started:

  1. Upload your laboratory report, blood test, or metabolic panel in the left sidebar.
  2. Click "Process Reports" to extract data (with automatic OCR for scanned documents) and generate an initial clinical analysis.
  3. Ask questions in plain language about your tests, reference intervals, or clinical terms.

All answers are cross-referenced with your report and verified medical literature.
